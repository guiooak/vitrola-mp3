# D13 · Favorite songs and albums

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Related:** —

## Context

The maintainer wants to mark favorites with a heart, both songs and whole albums.

## Decision

Songs and albums can be favorited with the heart, in two independent lists (Playlists › Favorite songs and Favorite albums). Favoriting an album does not favorite its songs.

## Consequences

- Details in [03-mockup.md](../design/03-mockup.md#favorites).
- "Unknown album" cannot be favorited.
