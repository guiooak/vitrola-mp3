The app's style guide: colors, typography, spacing, components and motion. Everything here comes from tokens.json, which feeds the mockup and, later, the app theme in Jetpack Compose.

01

Principles

Four ideas that settle any design decision.

local_fire_department

Analog warmth

Cream, wine and mustard instead of cold gray. It should feel like a living room with a turntable, not a control panel.

album

Music comes first

The cover and the record are the stars. The interface steps back: few buttons and no decoration competing with the artwork.

touch_app

As simple as putting on a record

Every common task (play, skip, find a song) takes two taps at most.

accessibility_new

For everyone

AA contrast, 48dp touch targets, respect for "reduce motion" and screen readers.

02

Colors

Three primitive scales (wine, mustard and warm neutrals) become role-based colors that switch between the light and dark themes. Components use only the role colors, never the primitives directly.

Primitive scales

Role colors

Pairings and contrast

Contrast is computed live for the current theme. The target is AA: 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and icons. Mustard is never used as a text color on light backgrounds, only as a fill with dark text on top.

03

Typography

Two families. Fraunces is the brand voice, used for titles, album names and the "now playing" screen. Outfit handles the whole interface: lists, buttons and times.

Brand · titles
Aa

Fraunces, a "soft" serif with an optical size axis. Weights 600–800. Use only at 22sp and up.

Interface · text
Aa

Outfit, geometric and very legible at small sizes. Weights 400–600. Tabular figures for times (03:41).

04

Space and shape

A 4dp grid. Generously rounded corners echo vinyl and the knobs of old stereos. Shadows are warm (brown, not black) and subtle.

Spacing

Radii

Elevation

In the dark theme, elevation shows through a lighter surface color more than through shadow.

05

Icons

Material Symbols Rounded, weight 400, 24dp. The rounded ends match Fraunces and the record. Outlined by default; filled for the active item (e.g. current tab, favorited item).

06

Components

The building blocks of the screens. They are interactive: click to see their states.

Buttons

Primary (wine): the main action, at most one per screen · Tonal: secondary actions · Accent (mustard): "play", use sparingly · Outline and text: low-emphasis actions.

Player controls

1:243:41
While playing, the play button morphs from a circle into a rounded square. Shuffle and repeat turn wine with a dot when active, and repeat cycles through off, all and one (icon repeat_one). The seek thumb is mustard, like the tonearm headshell.

"Now playing" record

The album cover becomes the record label. The record spins at 33⅓ rpm (one turn every 1.8s), like an LP. When paused it slows down to a stop instead of freezing.

Library filters

Song list

The current song shows its title in wine and animated equalizer bars over the cover. Click another song to switch.

Albums and fallback covers

When an MP3 has no cover, the app generates one from the album name: a palette color, grooves and initials in Fraunces. The same album always gets the same cover.

07

Motion

Motion mimics the physical: the record has weight, the arm lowers slowly, panels "settle". Nothing blinks or bounces.

Durations

Standard easing

Used for most transitions: starts fast and settles gently.

Mini player → full screen

The mini player grows into the "now playing" screen (decelerate easing, 500ms).

With "reduce motion" turned on, the record does not spin and transitions become a quick fade.

08

Voice and tone

Short sentences in the tone of a friend who knows music. The turntable metaphor shows up in special moments (empty state, welcome), never on buttons or actions.

09

Accessibility

  • check_circle
    AA contrast for all text (4.5:1) and icons (3:1), in both themes. See the color table.
  • check_circle
    48dp touch targets at minimum, even when the icon is smaller.
  • check_circle
    Screen reader labels on every icon-only button ("Next song", not "skip_next").
  • check_circle
    Respect "reduce motion": the record stays still and transitions get short.
  • check_circle
    Scalable text: everything in sp, and the layout handles 200% font size without clipping titles (ellipsis when needed).
  • check_circle
    State never relies on color alone: active shuffle and repeat get a dot, and the selected chip gets a ✓.
