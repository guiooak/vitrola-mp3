Vitrola — logo concepts

Four directions, from the most retro to the most minimal. Each one is shown as an app icon, at small sizes (legibility test), in Android's round mask and next to the name. Hover over the icons to see the record spin.

Concept A · Retro

Classic vinyl

The grooved record, wine label and the tonearm with the needle playing. It is the most literal "turntable" and ties straight into the spinning "now playing" screen.

How to choose

  1. Pick a concept, or combine parts (e.g. "symbol from A with the typeface from C").
  2. Say what to adjust: colors, stroke weights, typeface, level of detail.
  3. Once chosen, the drawing gets refined and the final files are generated: SVG, Android adaptive icon and monochrome version.
