Vitrola Mp3 — logo

Concept A, refined: the classic vinyl record. This page brings together the symbol, its variations for each size, the Android icon, the lockups with the name, and the base palette. It is the reference for the style guide.

Symbol

A turntable at a glance

  • Record with alternating grooves and two highlights that add depth without gradients.
  • Wine label with a mustard ring, the brand's color signature.
  • Tonearm with counterweight, base and headshell. A soft shadow lifts the arm off the record.
  • A light rim keeps the record visible on dark backgrounds.

The button previews the animation planned for the app: the arm drops and the record spins.

Construction

Record
Radius 70 on a 200 grid. Shifted left and down to make room for the arm.
Label
Radius 25, about 1/3 of the record, like a real LP.
Tonearm
Runs from the pivot (top right corner) to the needle on the outer third of the record, where the music starts.
Stroke
Arm stroke is 7 in the detailed version and 10 in the simplified one.

Sizes: detailed × simplified

Below 48px the detailed version turns into noise. The simplified one has fewer grooves, thicker strokes and a larger label. Use it in the status bar, notifications and favicon.

Android adaptive icon

Each manufacturer crops the icon into a different shape. The symbol sits inside the central safe zone (66 of 108 units) and stays whole in every mask.

Themed icon (Android 13+)

A single-color version that Android tints to match the user's wallpaper. It is also used for the playback notification.

Lockups

Name: Vitrola in Fraunces ExtraBold and Mp3 in Fraunces Medium, at the same size. The weight contrast creates hierarchy without a second color. Fraunces is a "soft" serif that feels like a 1970s record sleeve while staying modern, and it is free (Google Fonts). In the exported files the text is already outlined and does not depend on installed fonts.
